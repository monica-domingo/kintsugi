# Organisms

Organisms are more complex UI components in the Kintsugi component library, following the Atomic Design methodology. They are typically composed of groups of Atoms and/or Molecules, combined to form distinct sections of an interface.

## Purpose

- **Complex Sections:** Organisms represent relatively complex, standalone sections of a user interface. Examples include page headers, footers, sidebars, product grids, or complex forms.
- **Composition:** They are built by assembling Atoms and Molecules into a functional and cohesive unit.
- **Contextual Structure:** Organisms provide structure to multiple related components, giving them a shared context.
- **Reusability at a Larger Scale:** While more specific than Atoms or Molecules, Organisms can still be reused across different parts of an application or even in different applications.

## Available Organisms

Currently, there are no organism components available in this category. Components will be added here as the library develops and Atoms and Molecules are assembled into larger, functional UI sections.

*(Examples of organisms that might be added: PageHeader, ProductGrid, CommentSection, ArticleCard, etc.)*
